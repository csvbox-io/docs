The **Custom Theme** option lets you personalize the appearance of the importer to match your brand. Adjust colors, fonts, and layout sizes to create a consistent, professional look.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the Custom Theme">
    Go to the importer sheet settings → **Display** tab and select the **Custom Theme** option under the **Importer Theme** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust styling parameters">
    Use the **left sidebar controls** to modify colors, fonts, sizing, and other styling options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your changes">
    Click **Preview** to instantly see how your changes affect the importer's appearance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and apply">
    Once you're satisfied with the customization, click **Save** to apply the theme.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download & reuse importer styling (CSS)

You can reuse the same importer styling across multiple sheets or projects. After customizing the look and feel of your importer, download the styling as a CSS file, then upload it to another importer to instantly apply the same design with no rework.

This is especially useful when you want consistent branding across multiple imports or environments.

### Download the styling file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customize your importer styling">
    Go to your importer's **Customize Styling** section and adjust colors, borders, typography, and other visual settings as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the styling file">
    Once you're happy with the design, click **Download CSS**. CSVbox saves your current styling as a reusable `.css` file.

    This file contains all the visual configurations you've applied to the importer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Reusing the styling on another importer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the CSS file">
    To apply the same styling to another sheet or importer:

    1. Open the **Customize Styling** section of the new importer.
    2. Click **Upload CSS**.
    3. Select the previously downloaded CSS file.

    The styling is applied instantly to the importer preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and save">
    Review the updated look in the preview, then click **Save** to persist the styling. Your importer now matches the original design.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Notes & best practices

- Uploading a CSS file **overwrites the current styling** of the importer.
- You can still make further adjustments after uploading.
- Always preview before saving to make sure everything looks correct.
- Keep a backup of your CSS files if you maintain multiple themes.
