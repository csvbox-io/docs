---

Import CSV files and Excel sheets directly into [Google Sheets](https://www.google.co.in/sheets/about/).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Google Sheets as the destination">
    Select **Google Sheets** as the destination type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Google account">
    Click the Google button and accept the relevant permissions.

    <Note>
      The importer requires permission to view the list of Google sheets in your account and to edit sheet data.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the target sheet">
    Provide the Google sheet name, and specify the worksheet name where you want the data to be pushed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map columns">
    Map the template columns to the Google sheet columns. You can also map custom attributes to sheet columns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your users' CSV data is then added directly to the Google sheet.
