---
title: "Notion"
description: "Import customer CSV data to your Notion database."
---

The Notion destination pushes the CSV and Excel files your users upload directly into a Notion database.

## Set up the destination

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to CSVbox">
    Log in to [csvbox.io](https://app.csvbox.io/login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a sheet">
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-25.png"
        alt="Add a sheet"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add columns">
    Add columns to the sheet as per your requirement.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-17.png"
        alt="Add columns to the sheet"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Notion as the destination">
    Under the **Destination** section, for the **Send Data To** setting, select **Notion**. Then click **Connect Notion**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-20.png"
        alt="Connect Notion button"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the integration">
    You are redirected to the Notion Integration Authorization screen.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-13.png"
        alt="Notion integration authorization screen"
      />
    </Frame>

    Select the page and the relevant workspace where you want the CSV data to be pushed.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-15.png"
        alt="Select Notion pages to share"
      />
    </Frame>

    Click **Allow access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the workspace with the integration">
    Log in to your Notion account and go to the workspace where you want to receive the CSV data. Click **Share** (top right of the dashboard) and share the workspace with the **CSV to Notion** integration.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/share.jpg"
        alt="Share workspace with CSVbox integration"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      The Notion database is visible in the CSVbox dashboard only after you share the workspace with the CSV to Notion integration.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the Notion database">
    Go back to your CSVbox sheet and pick the Notion database from the list.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-27.png"
        alt="Select the Notion database"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map columns">
    Click **Map Columns** and map the sheet columns to the Notion database columns.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-21.png"
        alt="Map sheet columns to Notion database columns"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the sheet">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CSVs uploaded by your users are now pushed to the Notion database.
