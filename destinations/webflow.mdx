---
title: "Webflow"
description: "Import customer CSV data to your Webflow CMS."
---

The Webflow destination pushes the CSV and Excel files your users upload directly into a Webflow CMS collection.

## Set up the destination

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to CSVbox">
    Log in to [csvbox.io](https://app.csvbox.io/login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a sheet">
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-25.png"
        alt="Add a sheet"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add columns">
    Add columns to the sheet as per your requirement.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-17.png"
        alt="Add columns to the sheet"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Webflow as the destination">
    Under the **Destination** section, for the **Send Data To** setting, select **Webflow**. Then click **Connect Webflow**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/connect-webflow.jpg"
        alt="Connect Webflow"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the application">
    You are redirected to the Webflow CSVbox Integration Authorization screen.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/webflow-auth.jpg"
        alt="Webflow authorization"
      />
    </Frame>

    Select the site where you want the CSV data to be pushed, then click **Authorize Application**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the site and collection">
    Go back to your CSVbox sheet and pick the Webflow site and the collection from the list.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/webflow-site-selection.jpg"
        alt="Select site and collection"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map columns">
    Click **Map Columns** and map the sheet columns to the Webflow CMS collection fields.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/col-mappings.jpg"
        alt="Column mapping"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the sheet">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CSVs uploaded by your users are now pushed to the Webflow CMS.
