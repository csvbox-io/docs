---
title: "Neon"
description: "Push your CSV files and Excel sheets directly into your Neon (serverless Postgres) database."
---

The Neon destination appends uploaded spreadsheet data from CSVbox straight into a table in your Neon serverless Postgres database.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Neon as the destination type">
    Open your sheet, go to **Destination**, and choose **Neon** from the list.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-38.png"
        alt="Neon selected as the destination type"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Neon database">
    Enter your Neon credentials: the host, database name, user, and password. You can copy these from your Neon project dashboard under **Connection Details**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/image-39.png"
        alt="Neon database connection credentials"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Specify the table name">
    Enter the name of the table where you want the data to be pushed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map sheet columns to table columns">
    Match each column in your sheet to the matching column in your Neon table. You can also map custom attributes to table columns.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once set up, your CSV data is appended directly to the Neon table.
