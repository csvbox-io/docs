---
title: "Zapier"
description: "Push user CSV data to Zapier as a trigger and move it to any connected app."
---

The Zapier destination pushes the data from files your users upload to Zapier as a trigger. You can then move this data to any connected app of your choice.

## Set up the destination

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to CSVbox">
    Log in to [csvbox.io](https://app.csvbox.io/login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a sheet">
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/add-sheet.png"
        alt="Add a sheet"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add columns">
    Add columns to the sheet as per your requirement.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/add-columns.png"
        alt="Add columns"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Zapier as the destination">
    Under the **Settings** section, for the **Send Data To** setting, select **Zapier**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a Zap">
    Click **Connect Zap**. This redirects you to Zapier.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/connect-zap.jpg"
        alt="Connect Zap"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new Zap">
    Log in to your Zapier account and create a new Zap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the CSVbox trigger">
    Select **csvbox.io** as the Trigger.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/csvbox-trigger.jpg"
        alt="csvbox.io trigger in Zapier"
      />
    </Frame>

    Select **New Import Row** as the Trigger Event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your CSVbox account">
    Provide your API Key and API Secret Key. You can find these keys on the **Accounts** page of your CSVbox dashboard.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/keys.jpg"
        alt="API credentials"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the sheet">
    Select the sheet (template) in the **sheets** dropdown of the **Set up Trigger** section. Data from this sheet is pushed to Zapier one row at a time.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/trigger-sheet.jpg"
        alt="Select sheet"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the trigger">
    Test the trigger and continue with setting up the Action of your Zap.
  </Step>
</Steps>
