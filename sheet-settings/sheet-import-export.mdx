<a id="sheet-import--export" />

Export a sheet from one CSVbox account and import it into another, or duplicate it within the same account, in a few clicks. Use it to:

- Move sheets between staging and production accounts
- Share templates across teams
- Back up sheet configurations
- Reuse validated sheet structures

## Export a sheet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your sheets">
    Go to **Sheets** in your dashboard and locate the sheet you want to export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Click the **Export** button in the **Actions** column. A dialog opens showing:

    - **Sheet License Key**
    - **Export Key**
    - Options to **Copy**, **Show**, or **Regenerate** the export key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy both keys">
    Copy both the **Sheet License Key** and the **Export Key**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import a sheet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the target account">
    Log in to the account where you want to import the sheet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Import Sheet">
    Click **Add Sheet**, then select **Import Sheet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the keys">
    Paste the **Sheet License Key** and the **Export Key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Import">
    Click **Import**. The entire sheet, including columns, validations, configuration, and settings, is copied into the new account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The imported sheet gets a **new License Key**. The original sheet, and its existing imports and data, are not affected.
</Note>
