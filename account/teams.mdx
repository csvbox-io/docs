---
title: "Teams"
description: "Organize and control access to CSVbox by adding team members."
---

Invite additional members of your team to your CSVbox dashboard from the [Team](https://app.csvbox.io/user/team) page.

When you invite a new team member, you're asked what role they serve in your organization. CSVbox limits dashboard functionality based on a user's role. An admin in your team can change any user's role at any time.

## Roles and access

The roles and the dashboard areas each one can access (at a broad level) are:

| Page | Super Admin | Admin | Tech | Finance |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Home | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Sheets | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Imports | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Plans | Yes | Yes | No | Yes |
| Settings | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Teams | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Each account has one super admin. The super admin can't be changed.

<Note>
  The maximum number of team members you can add depends on your [plan](https://csvbox.io/#pricing).
</Note>
